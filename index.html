Features

Mirror, control, and debug.

A fast, reliable window to your device with the input and workflow you already use.

Mirror & control

See and touch your iOS or Android device from your desktop with full control.

Audio streaming

Hear your Android or iPhone through your desktop speakers, in sync with the screen.

AI automation (MCP)

Point any MCP client at Vysor and let an LLM screenshot, tap, swipe, type, and navigate—agentic QA without an emulator.

Go wireless

Cut the cord and connect over Wi-Fi so you can roam while you stay mirrored.

Vysor Share

Share an Android or iOS device — screen and control — across the office or across the globe, as simple as sending a link.

Drag & drop files

Move files between your desktop and device in either direction with a simple drag.

Developers

Ditch the emulator.

Vysor gives you the integration and ease of an emulator on a physical device. Test on real hardware, share it with your team, and automate with ADB or an AI agent over MCP.

  • Real device, real results. Integration and ease of an emulator on physical hardware.
  • Remote ADB access. Share screen and ADB with teammates anywhere in the world.
  • Agent-ready via MCP. Point an AI agent or MCP client at a live device to screenshot, tap, swipe, type, and navigate.
  • Embed anywhere. Drop a live device into any website or test tool via iframe.
